# Roadmap

The [README](../README.md) names the directions. This page keeps the ideas behind them. None has a date, and
each keeps one promise: Sextile casts nothing. Astrolog does.

Through 0.1.x the work goes mostly into how Sextile feels to use. Small steps improve how it keeps your work.

- **Everyday reads, one key away.** Aspect patterns, dignities, midpoints, the lunar phase, risings and settings,
  each as a tab.
- **When is it exact?** One key for the last or next exact aspect, and a timeline over any span.
- **Speak Astrolog directly.** Type a switch and it opens as a tab. Copy any tab out as one Astrolog command.
- **Charts with children.** Progressions, returns, relocations and rectification variants, linked to the chart they
  came from.
- **Ways of looking.** House system, zodiac or a heliocentric view, kept as named views that survive a restart.
- **More traditions.** Techniques as files: horary, time lords, Munich Rhythm, Uranian, each checked against a
  cited source.
- **Astrolog's own wheel in the terminal**, including Vedic chart forms.
- **1.0: a release you can cite**, with sessions you can replay and exports that carry their settings.
