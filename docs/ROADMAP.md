# What may come next

This page lists the plans for Sextile. Nothing here has a date, and plans change.

For what is already there, see [what changed in each release](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Next

These are the next steps, in this order.

- **Each session keeps its own charts.** Opening a chart from your charts folder takes a copy into the session.
  The file in your charts folder stays as it was.
- **The list of charts in three groups.** This session's charts, the charts of your other sessions, and your
  charts folder.
- **Derived charts.** A chart gets rows under it: the same person or event at another moment or place. You steer
  one with the time keys, compare it with its chart, and keep it as a chart of its own. This is for a corrected
  birth time and for choosing a date.
- **Notes on a chart.** A few lines of your own, kept beside the chart.
- **Each chart's own bodies in the wheel.** The bodies that you select for a chart are the ones on its ring,
  also when several charts share one wheel. Each chart can show a different set.

## Later, in no fixed order

- **Enter birth data.** Today you reach a moment with the time keys. One form shall take a date, a time and a
  place, as numbers or from the list of cities.
- **The first start asks for your city.** Today Sextile takes the city from your computer's time zone, and
  says so in the Log only. It shall ask once and keep your answer.
- **Your settings, where they belong.** A chart's houses, zodiac and Nodes written into the chart's own file.
  Your own calculation settings brought in, where they are a matter of taste.
- **The true Node with the mean Lilith.** Today the list of forms holds the four pairs that Astrolog sets with
  its two switches. The pair that many programs show shall join them.
- **More kinds of derived charts.** A return, a progression, and a corrected time written back into the chart's
  own file, with the old time kept.
- **The wheel in a picture viewer.** Today the wheel opens in your browser. On a Linux desktop it shall open in a
  picture viewer, with nothing served.
- **A start on a Mac that is tested.** Today a Mac is expected to work, but that is not tested.

## Ideas, in no order

Each of these builds on calculations that are already there.

- **Interpretation as a study aid.** Short texts on a chart's placements and aspects, beside the numbers, to
  help your study.
- **More everyday readings as tabs.** Aspect patterns, dignities, midpoints, risings and settings. The Moon has
  its tab already.
- **When is it exact?** One step to the last or the next exact aspect. A timeline over any span, not only one
  month.
- **Speak to the calculation directly.** Type a switch and read the result as a tab. Copy any tab as one
  command.
- **Ways of looking.** A heliocentric view. Houses, zodiac and planets kept together as a named view.
- **More traditions.** Techniques as files, each checked against a cited source: horary, time lords, Munich
  Rhythm Theory, Uranian astrology.
- **More pictures.** Other chart forms, Vedic ones among them.
- **1.0: a release you can cite.** Sessions that you can replay, and exports that carry their settings.

## What is not planned

- **Guessed numbers.** Where the calculation cannot give a value, Sextile shows none.
- **A phone app or an account.** Sextile runs on your computer and sends nothing.

Do you miss something? Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/cruiserxyz/sextile/issues) and say what you
wanted to do.
